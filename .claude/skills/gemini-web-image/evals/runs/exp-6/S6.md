  `prompt box never appeared at <url>` row: "Read the URL. `google.com/sorry/...`
  is Google's challenge and `accounts.google.com` is a sign-in wall — clear it

  matches the script's own `CHALLENGE_RE` (`google\.com/sorry/|accounts\.google\.com`)

- Do not switch to another image backend (e.g. a CLI/API path) from inside
